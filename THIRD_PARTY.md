# UnitTestFramework

`unittestframework/` contains the [OpenROAD UnitTestFramework](https://github.com/ActianCorp/OpenROAD_UnitTestFramework),
licensed under [Apache 2.0](unittestframework/LICENSE). Its source comments credit
Bodo Bergmann and are retained.

This copy was converted from an installed framework export to Gorak's readable
format. Trailing whitespace and mixed indentation were normalized; framework
behavior was not changed. The export did not record an upstream version.

The root MIT license applies to the original demo, not the framework.
